Lays out a `Container`'s children side by side, from left to right, or from right to left with the container's `direction: "rtl"`. Children with a percent width share the width the others leave, in proportion to their percents.

Docs: https://docs.amcharts.com/v5/concepts/common-elements/containers/#layout

## Import

```js
import * as am5 from "@amcharts/amcharts5";

am5.HorizontalLayout.new(root, { /* settings */ });
```
