"Spirited Dark": the `Spirited` palette on a dark background, with neutral dark interface colors. Colors too dark to show on the background are lightened. Use it in place of `Spirited`.

Docs: https://www.amcharts.com/docs/v5/concepts/themes/

## Import

```js
import am5themes_SpiritedDark from "@amcharts/amcharts5/themes/SpiritedDark";

root.setThemes([am5themes_SpiritedDark.new(root)]);
```
